## Three working formalisms

1. Inverse/identification problem: an observation channel connects latent processes to recorded traces. Distinguish observational equivalence, regularization, empirical restriction, and value judgment.
2. Representational measurement: preserve empirical relations and ask which claims survive admissible transformations. Ordinal order does not establish equal gaps. Counterexample: A=[1,4], B=[2,3]. Both arithmetic means 2.5; strictly increasing f_q(s)=(s^q-1)/(4^q-1) flips their mean ranking for q below/above one while retaining anchors/order. This is an illustration of known measurement theory, not a new finding. Medians can be ambiguous with even n; use pairwise superiority/rank facts as invariant examples.
3. Robust decisions over model and value sets: retain a vector/set until a scalar and its trade-offs have justification. Report inf/sup over compatible models and declared weights. Refusal to rank is different from equality.

Working practical example: two synthetic support-service designs, described on a 0–100 scenario rubric for access and participant control. A has access [75,85] and control [25,45]; B has access [45,65] and control [65,85]. Anchors, comparability, meaningful differences, and additive value are assumptions of the illustration, not validated instruments for autonomy or dignity.

With access weight w, the fixed-weight score-gap interval A−B is [70w−60,60w−20]. For allowed weights [lo,hi], its extrema are [70lo−60,60hi−20] for these profiles. At w=.5 the range is [-25,10]. Midpoint profiles A=(80,35), B=(55,75) produce scores57.5 and65, a gap−7.5. At w=.9 the gap[3,34] robustly favors A; at w=.1 the gap[-53,-14] favors B. All weights [0,1] give[-60,40], an unresolved ranking. These are not sampling confidence intervals.

The optional audit narrows A control to[30,40] and B control to[70,80], leaving access unchanged. A nonempty nested compatible set cannot widen a gap interval under the same value set and scoring rule. Evidence contradicting prior model assumptions can instead expand or replace the model class. The refinement claim is elementary set inclusion, not a novel theorem.
