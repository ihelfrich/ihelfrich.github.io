## Three working formalisms

1. Inverse/identification problem: an observation channel connects latent processes to recorded traces. Distinguish observational equivalence, regularization, empirical restriction, and value judgment.
2. Representational measurement: preserve empirical relations and ask which claims survive admissible transformations. Ordinal order does not establish equal gaps. Counterexample: A=[1,4], B=[2,3]. Both arithmetic means 2.5; strictly increasing f_q(s)=(s^q-1)/(4^q-1) flips their mean ranking for q below/above one while retaining anchors/order. This is an illustration of known measurement theory, not a new finding. Medians can be ambiguous with even n; use pairwise superiority/rank facts as invariant examples.
3. Robust decisions over model and value sets: retain a vector/set until a scalar and its trade-offs have justification. Report inf/sup over compatible models and declared weights. Refusal to rank is different from equality.
