# Introductory econometrics draft review

30 original single-best-answer questions: five in each of six units. All remain `draft`. The source URLs are official curriculum anchors from `docs/econ-practice/research/fields-curriculum.md`; they establish topical provenance, not validation of these original questions or any empirical finding.

## Coverage

1. Data and estimands: sampling frames, association, panel structure, potential outcomes, attrition.
2. Simple OLS: slope units, residuals, least-squares criterion, intercept extrapolation, rescaling.
3. Multiple regression: omitted-variable bias, partial associations, mediator controls, exact and imperfect collinearity.
4. Inference: confidence intervals, p-values, heteroskedasticity, school clustering, imprecision.
5. Functional forms and prediction: log-level and log-log models, interactions, prediction intervals, validation loss.
6. Introductory causal designs: randomized offers, difference in differences, regression discontinuity, instrument exclusion, fixed effects.

## Review and corrections

Self-reviewed every correct answer, all three distractors, and each explanation for a unique defensible key under the prompt assumptions. Checked IDs, four distinct choices per question, draft status, and five questions per unit. Independently recomputed the residual (−60), interval endpoints (1.02 and 2.98), exact log change (4.08%), interaction slope (2.5), and difference-in-differences contrast (5 percentage points) with executable arithmetic assertions.

During drafting, made the log-outcome target explicitly the exponentiated fitted value rather than mean rent, to avoid a retransformation error. Made log-log interpretation local rather than claiming an exact finite percentage change. Specified population omitted-variable bias, a conditionally mean-zero remainder, and both sign assumptions. Specified many independent school clusters and complete randomized-offer outcomes with no interference. Distinguished mediator adjustment from identification of a direct effect. Added nonzero outcome noise to the prediction-interval comparison and qualified validation choice as evidence rather than a guarantee. These are incorporated clarifications, not unresolved corrections.

## Limits and next gate
