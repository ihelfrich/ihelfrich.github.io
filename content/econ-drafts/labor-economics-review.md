# Labor economics draft review

Thirty original choice items, five in each of six units. All remain `draft`; none are approved or activated. The MIT Fall 2021 labor syllabus was opened and used as a curriculum anchor, not as a source of copied questions or empirical estimates. Numerical scenarios are invented teaching examples; no item attributes an invented estimate to actual research.

Reviewed every key against its assumptions and three distractors. Independently checked childcare participation ($10), transfer phaseout ($15), bakery marginal profit ($20), elasticity (−2%), competitive floor (60 employed), certificate NPV ($0), signaling net returns ($3,000 versus −$3,000), flexibility sorting ($4,000 salary cost), monopsony (10 workers), monopsony wage floor (15 workers), steady unemployment (10%), difference-in-differences ($40), and Wald estimate ($60).

Ambiguity controls: inheritance holds wages fixed and specifies normal leisure; temporary-wage substitution holds lifetime utility fixed; payroll incidence specifies perfectly inelastic supply and flexible wages; signaling asks only incentive compatibility given wages, without claiming a complete equilibrium; union bargaining specifies right-to-manage; migration fixes capital and demand and assumes perfect substitutes. The search item was tightened during review to require positive acceptance probability and constant independent offer arrivals, excluding the zero-hazard exception. Audit-study, wage-decomposition, college-selection, and regression-discontinuity explanations explicitly limit the inference.

Structural audit `node scripts/econ-practice/audit-drafts.mjs` passed with zero errors after creation. JSON-level assertions also verified 30 unique IDs, five items per unit, and four distinct answer strings per item. Parent content review remains required. Coverage is a compact sample, not a complete advanced labor curriculum; task models, bargaining derivations, equilibrium matching and heterogeneous migration remain expansion opportunities. The shared official source URL is a broad curriculum anchor, not item-specific proof of each derivation.
