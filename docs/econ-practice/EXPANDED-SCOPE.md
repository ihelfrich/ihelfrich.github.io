# Economics platform: expanded requirements

Dr. Helfrich's latest instruction expands the active project to more than 5,000 active, useful practice questions spanning economics disciplines. It supersedes the earlier bank-size target without removing the original AP Micro requirement, psychometric integrity, response tracking, instructor access, deployment, design, or review requirements.

Organize learning by general course titles: Principles of Microeconomics and Macroeconomics; Intermediate and Advanced Microeconomics and Macroeconomics; Econometrics and other methods; economics field courses. Preserve AP preparation as recognizable offerings. Distinguish graduate models from undergraduate prerequisites rather than placing them in one undifferentiated track.

Official research-university syllabi and course materials will establish a documented coverage map. “Every model, theory, and concept” is a breadth objective, not a claim that a finite sample of syllabi proves universal coverage. Record source institutions, course level, model assumptions, omissions, and the date inspected. Separate normative viewpoints, competing theories, accounting identities, causal claims, and empirical findings.

The acceptance count is more than 5,000 released, usable items, not drafts, cosmetic paraphrases, or catalog entries. Report distinct families and variants separately. Track coverage by concept and cognitive task: calculation, interpretation, comparative statics, assumptions/counterexamples, empirical identification, policy tradeoffs, graphs, and multi-step reasoning. Do not inflate counts by increasing a generator's variant loop without checking instructional value and answer variation.

An operational item needs a stable ID/version, course/concept tags, prerequisite level, prompt, valid scoring method, key/rubric, explanation, suitable guidance, content-review evidence and a usable rendering. Empirical calibration is separate from substantive review; initially released practice may use explicitly provisional author parameters. No common ability scale across unrelated courses without dimensionality and linking evidence.

Execution: three bounded, non-overlapping research agents cover micro theory, macro theory, and fields/methods. The parent owns code integration and reconciles the taxonomy before assigning item-authoring batches. Agents write separate source files. Reuse shared references and mechanical validators; use independent equation checks and representative-plus-boundary review for parameterized families. Do not create extra chats or duplicate tracking systems.

Current baseline before expansion: 2,439 rendered items / 232 families, including 1,316 AP Micro items. These are not yet evidence of 5,000 active reviewed items. Remaining operational/deployment requirements remain open.
