# Econometrics Across Disciplines: development plan

This is the seventy-two-chapter plan that /handbook/ described until October 2026. The public page now describes only the working edition, which has three foundation chapters. The plan is kept here as a planning record; it is not a publication commitment. The text below is copied from `src/pages/handbook/index.astro` at commit 517991bc (lines 33 to 44), with the HTML removed.

## The developing manuscript

1. **Objects and foundations, chapters 1–9.** Economic questions, measurement, observation clocks, probability, geometry, calculus, sampling, identification, and decisions.
2. **Econometric inference, chapters 10–21.** Regression, uncertainty, instruments, likelihood and moments, nonlinear outcomes, distributions, missing data, panels, causal designs, and structural behavior.
3. **Dynamic systems, chapters 22–36.** Prediction, signals, stochastic trends, latent states, multivariate systems, identification, cointegration, risk, breaks, spectra, and mixed-frequency information. The current time-series lectures (/time-series/#contents) supply material for this part; broader-book revisions and prerequisite links remain to be written.
4. **Geography, networks, and measurement, chapters 37–48.** Geographic support, spatial dependence, network mechanisms, functional data, latent constructs, indexes, changing measurement, topology, and formal connections across disciplines.
5. **Advanced foundations, chapters 49–60.** Doctoral probability, dependent-data asymptotics, empirical processes, weak and partial identification, semiparametric and functional methods, high dimensions, Bayesian and simulation methods, nonstandard limits, and continuous time.
6. **Computation and professional work, chapters 61–66.** Three-language analysis, verification, AI-assisted reasoning, AI measurement, communication, and reproducible production.
7. **Research investigations, chapters 67–72.** Representation loss, scale, evolving networks, constrained distributions, adversarial audit, and precisely bounded research questions. Novelty and open-problem status require a literature review for each claim.

Thirty applied cases are specified in the development plan. The observed Texas forecasting case is implemented. Chapter 3 adds the audited release-ledger prerequisite for the real-time GDP case; the full nowcast case and other planned cases remain in development. The controlled examples are foundation models, not additional completed empirical cases.
