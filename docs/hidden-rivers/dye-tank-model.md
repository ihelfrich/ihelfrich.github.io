# Salinity dye tank, model register v3

**Scope:** A browser-resident two-dimensional, closed 0.24 × 0.18 m tank at fixed temperature. A dyed patch replaces water locally, with no initial momentum. It illustrates buoyancy, plume circulation and mixing for the Hidden Rivers lesson. Model/schema version 3: TEOS-10 buoyancy, improved transport and tank-wall conditions. Classification: physical approximation plus an explicitly separate artistic display. It does not use measured ocean currents.

## Implemented contracts

State coordinates are x rightward and y downward, in metres. The staggered MAC grid stores u on vertical faces and v on horizontal faces, in m/s. Salinity S is cell-centred in g/kg; pigment fractions are dimensionless. Time is seconds. Pressure projection uses velocity potential φ in m²/s; the perturbation pressure divided by reference density is φ/Δt, in m²/s². The column's hydrostatic component is removed analytically by subtracting each row's mean density from buoyancy.

| State | Class | Domain and invariant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| u, v | Intensive velocity fields | Finite; normal velocity exactly zero on all four walls |
| S | Intensive concentration | Initial and drop values 0–40 g/kg; source-free integral ∑S Δx Δy conserved |
| Gold and coral pigment | Intensive tracers | Nonnegative up to numerical tolerance; each source-free integral conserved |
| Total dye | Derived observation | Gold + coral at every cell; no independently advected total that can contradict a pigment |
| Integral of concentration × area | Extensive 2D tracer store | In g/kg·m² for salt; m² for pigment. These are not kilograms of salt |
| Drop | External control | x,y inside tank; radius 2–25 mm; click or drag applies discrete mixing/replacement events recorded in the integral ledger; dragging does not inject momentum |
| Rendering | Artistic display | Gold for saltier or matching additions, coral for fresher additions; nonlinear optical mapping. No feedback into physics |

Boussinesq momentum is ∂u/∂t + u·∇u = −∇(p/ρ₀) + ν∇²u + g(ρ(S) − horizontal mean ρ)/ρ₀ e_y, with ∇·u = 0. The inertial reference ρ₀ = 1022.943497 kg/m³ is TEOS-10 water at S_A = 30 g/kg, t = 10 °C and sea pressure 0 dbar. Density ρ(S) uses `gsw.rho_t_exact(S_A,10,0)` from GSW 3.6.23, tabulated over 0–40 g/kg at 0.1 g/kg intervals. A 16,001-point comparison bounds interpolation error below 0.00003 kg/m³; this is lookup error, not empirical uncertainty. Standard seawater composition and constant temperature are assumed. [Density table and provenance](/hidden-rivers/dye-tank/seawater.json).

Salinity and passive dye satisfy ∂q/∂t + ∇·(u q) = κ∇²q. ν = 1.3 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s is a rounded water-scale kinematic viscosity near 10 °C; salinity dependence is not resolved. κ = 2 × 10⁻⁸ m²/s is an effective smoothing diffusivity, ten times smaller than v2 but still greater than molecular salt diffusion. Grid transport also causes numerical diffusion.

Cadence/order: deterministic inputs; limited MacCormack momentum transport using midpoint forward/backward traces; explicit viscosity; buoyancy; pressure projection; conservative scalar transport. Reversing advection estimates leading transport error. The correction is limited to departure-cell extrema before viscosity; it does not inject vorticity. Scalars use velocity-oriented Koren-limited MUSCL face reconstruction and SSP-RK3. Each interior face transfer is applied equally/oppositely to its neighbours. Gold and coral are transported as separate pigments, then summed into total dye. This keeps a pigment from numerically exceeding the total. A species that has never been injected stays exactly zero and needs no transport solve. Reconstruction reduces order near extrema; no third-order claim is made for the complete coupled solver. Fixed user steps remain 0.01 s with Courant and diffusion subdivisions.

All boundaries have zero scalar flux and zero normal velocity. Glass sides and bottom use no-slip viscous ghost values. The flat top has zero tangential shear under a rigid-lid approximation. No air-water interface is solved. Analytic vortex fixtures use all-free-slip walls to match their exact solution. Boundary-layer thickness and surface deformation are not claimed resolved.

Projection diagonalizes the cell-centred closed-wall negative Laplacian with separable DCT-II/III transforms on power-of-two grids. Eigenvalues are 4 sin²(π i / 2Nₓ) / Δx² + 4 sin²(π j / 2Nᵧ) / Δy². The zero mode is removed; the computed residual is checked against the original discrete operator. The browser uses 256 × 128 cells (0.9375 × 1.40625 mm), 2.67 times the previous cell count. Other verification grids retain the Jacobi-preconditioned conjugate-gradient reference solver with a target divergence RMS of 10⁻⁸ s⁻¹ and 400 iteration cap. Both solvers stop with an error above 10⁻⁶ s⁻¹. Float64 is canonical; transferred Float32 snapshots are display-only. No random forcing, vortex confinement, dye fade, imposed spiral, fixed sinking trajectory, or unrecorded momentum injection is used. Inputs and fixed steps determine replay; wall-clock pacing only decides how many fixed steps to request. Batches contain at most two steps. Completion callbacks start the next available batch without waiting for a screen repaint; shared credit accounting prevents double-counting and idle-time catch-up.

Sanity checks: every momentum term is m/s²; (ρ − mean ρ)/ρ₀ is dimensionless and positive for denser water; y-down acceleration therefore sinks dense water. κ∇²q has concentration/s units. Uniform salinity and a matching-salinity drop give zero velocity. Horizontally uniform stable stratification remains hydrostatic. In zero flow, symmetric diffusion leaves a centred drop's centroid unchanged. Normal walls prevent net fluid or tracer exchange. Conservative scalar integrals are checked against the explicit replacement ledger, not against their values before dye addition.

## Explicit non-claims

A qualitative 2D Boussinesq illustration, not a laboratory-calibrated prediction, direct numerical simulation of turbulence, three-dimensional vortex-ring calculation, free-surface drop impact, molecular mixing calculation, or model of any particular ocean overflow. The 0–40 g/kg control range demonstrates direction; TEOS-10 density does not remove the constant-inertia, constant-viscosity and 2D approximations. Screen interpolation does not create physical resolution. Animation runs at the display refresh cadence independently of worker messages. The renderer blends resolved scalar snapshots continuously; nonnegative cubic B-spline sampling smooths spatial contours without overshoot. Float-texture filtering is checked, with manual bilinear and Canvas fallbacks. The pigment palette, absorption curve, vignette and light falloff are artistic, not measured optics. Paused and invisible tanks stop continuous rendering and advancement. Slower devices may advance fewer physical seconds per wall-clock second; the clock shows simulated time. The separate TEOS-10 calculator uses Practical Salinity and its documented reference location; the tank uses Absolute Salinity in g/kg with a standard-composition density lookup.

## Failure behavior

Reject nonfinite/out-of-domain inputs before mutation. Freeze the browser tank and display an actionable reset message on non-convergence, a substep below 10⁻⁶ s, or worker failure; do not substitute decorative animation. Pausing, hiding the tab, and leaving the Prints tab stop advancement. Reset reconstructs the initial water field and its ledger. Resolution changes require a reset; no aggregation or invented detail is transferred.

## Verification entry points

`node --test tests/unit/dye-tank.test.mjs` covers buoyancy direction, both rotation signs, pressure divergence, scalar conservation/bounds, hydrostatic equilibrium, neutral drops, deterministic replay and invalid input atomicity. Preregistered moment checks: same centred 9 mm drop, 30 g/kg water/36 g/kg drop, 1.2 simulated seconds; grid sizes 48×36, 72×54, 96×72 and timesteps 0.01/0.005 s. Accepted centroid differences: coarse vs fine <3 mm, medium vs fine <2 mm, timestep halving <1 mm. This verifies global moments at this time, not convergence of fine vortex morphology. Results are saved in `dye-tank-verification.json` alongside long-run extremes and divergence/mass residuals.

`node scripts/hidden-rivers/verify-dye-tank.mjs` reproduces quantitative evidence. Spectral/reference agreement is tested on arbitrary closed-wall velocity fields, including an idempotence check. New-grid checks compare global moments against a finer 256 × 256 grid and halve the timestep, accepting centroid differences below 2 mm and 1 mm respectively. Long-run default and extreme salinities are also sampled at browser resolution. These checks do not establish convergence of fine vortex morphology.

New analytic criteria were fixed before transport implementation: a 64² free-slip Taylor-Green vortex (U = 0.04 m/s, square side 0.24 m, t = 1 s) must lose <0.5% energy beyond exp(−4νk²t), with smaller error at 128²; a 64² Gaussian dye patch translated 0.02 m in one second must have normalized L1 error <10%, nonnegative values and mass residual <10⁻¹² m². Verification also reports finer-grid tracer error and timestep halving. Density references and glass/surface boundary limits are checked in `tests/unit/dye-tank-accuracy.test.mjs`. A two-pigment 2.5-second mixing fixture checks each pigment integral and coral ≤ total dye; the independent-total approach was observed to violate this by 0.0106 concentration units. These are analytic/numerical fixtures, not laboratory calibration.

Browser release checks exercise salinity presets, click/drop interaction, pause, visible evolution, zero-text presentation and the legacy density anchor. Independent experimental and out-of-sample validation have not been performed.
