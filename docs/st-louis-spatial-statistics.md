# Local parcel statistics model contract

Version `st-louis-spatial-statistics-v1`. Classification: deterministic descriptive spatial summary of an official dated City parcel snapshot, not a valuation or forecast. No training, model coefficient, demographic inference or private market denominator.

Input center: WGS84 longitude/latitude from the selected exact official property evidence; radius must be 250 or 500 metres. Geometry inclusion: source Polygon/MultiPolygon intersects the radius in a local equirectangular projection centered at that selected point, Earth radius 6,371,008.8 m. Holes are honored. This local approximation is for neighborhood summaries, not legal boundary measurement. Intersect all expanded source tile bounds, never the ownership grid alone. No representative-point or centroid approximation.

Transport contract: existing same-origin City parcel manifest and bounded intersecting geometry tiles only. Maximum 12 tiles, 15,000,000 actual response bytes including manifest, and 5,000 matched distinct source records. No address-search index, background citywide download or silent truncation. An exceeded transport or record limit fails the entire summary; all required tile responses must succeed before statistics are returned. Source-invalid topology is excluded and counted; source snapshot geometry validation is preserved, not repaired.

Identity: deduplicate tiles on exact `recordKey`; count source accounts separately from distinct HANDLEs. Shared HANDLE accounts remain separate observations in the assessed-account median and raw land-use frequencies. No sum of assessment or lot area is exposed, because duplicate/shared lot accounts can make totals misleading. Source lot-area median is reported on distinct HANDLE groups only when all nonnull source areas in that group agree; conflicting groups are excluded and counted. Unknown/null and numeric zero are distinct. Assessment year is unknown.

Reproducibility: deterministic sorted tile URLs, recordKeys, HANDLEs and land-use codes; no clock-dependent arithmetic. USD levels for assessment, ft² for source lot area, metres for radius, integer counts. Optional public LRA snapshot is joined on both HANDLE/parcelKey and ParcelId. Counts describe that supplied subset only; no citywide/private market share. Coverage crossing the City boundary is explicitly partial geographic coverage, even if every intersecting City tile loaded.

Invalid input, manifest, source identity collision, tile failure or budget exhaustion produces no new statistics. A selection change clears prior displayed results and aborts/invalidates prior async work. An empty completed neighborhood has count zero and null medians. Doubling USD values doubles the assessed-account median; reordering or duplicating identical tile records leaves the result unchanged. Negative or nonfinite source assessment/area is unknown. This summary implies no transaction price, price growth, entitlement, ownership, availability or demographic attribute.

Validation: invariant-first unit tests cover geometry intersection/holes, radius boundaries, deduplication/account groups, missingness/zero, deterministic medians/frequencies, scale invariance, public-subset matching, City-edge coverage, transport and record limits, invalid topology, cancellation and failed tiles. Runtime evidence remains dated at the source retrieval timestamp; computation time is not a data update.
