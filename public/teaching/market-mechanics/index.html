Exact for these linear curves while trade remains positive. For nonlinear markets, the elasticity formula is generally a small-tax approximation. At zero trade, per-unit incidence is not uniquely identified.
Model boundaries. Competitive market; linear inverse demand and supply; quantities cannot be negative. Price controls assume efficient rationing, lowest-cost production, no government purchases, and no waiting, enforcement, or search costs. Under inefficient rationing, welfare can be lower. The shaded areas are model results, not universal policy forecasts. Sliders change the model; no personal data is saved or transmitted. No external libraries or remote calculation services. Replay shows a sequence of solved markets, not a model of real-time price adjustment.
