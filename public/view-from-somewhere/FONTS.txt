The type on the pages of The View from Somewhere is Latin Modern.

The Latin Modern Family of Fonts, version 2.005 (21 III 2021), by Boguslaw Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki
on behalf of TeX Users Groups, after the Computer Modern fonts designed by Donald E. Knuth.
Copyright 2003-2021 by B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki. Released under the GUST Font License, below.
The unmodified fonts: http://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/latin-modern/

What is distributed here is modified. The style sheet book.css in this folder embeds subsets of
5 of the fonts (lmroman10-regular, lmroman10-italic, lmroman10-bold, lmromancaps10-regular, lmmono10-regular),
reduced to Latin text, punctuation, arrows, the minus sign and the euro sign, converted to WOFF2 without
hinting, and declared under the family names "LM Roman", "LM Roman Caps" and "LM Mono".
Of the fonts' OpenType layout features only these are kept: kern, liga, clig, calt, ccmp, mark, mkmk.
The names inside the font files are unchanged. The license asks, without requiring it, that a modified
font be renamed; the pages refer to the subsets only by the family names above.
