PhD in Economics from Georgia Tech, 2024, under Tibor Besedes and Usha Nair-Reichert. Primary field international trade, secondary fields geospatial economics, microeconomic theory, and networks. The dissertation combined network centrality, spatial heterogeneity, and optimal transport in international trade. The methods turned out to travel: the same decomposition that handles a trade-network shock handles a place-based-credit program (Helfrich 2026 on the U.S. NMTC), and the same Wasserstein-space machinery handles the closed-loop observation problem at the heart of the [Penumbra program](/program).

Twin master's at Georgia Tech: Economics (2019, network effects in gravity models) and Geographic Information Science and Technology (2022, remote detection of economic growth and environmental health using multi- and hyper-spectral remote sensing). Before that, MA in Economics from Indiana (2017, international trade, game theory, financial networks); MSc in Economics and Public Policy at the Barcelona School of Economics (2015, thesis on the Pico y Placa driving restrictions in Quito, Ecuador, public-policy thesis-competition finalist); BA in Economics with Political Science from UNC Chapel Hill (2014).

Teaching at Georgia Tech as instructor of record on Econ 2106 (Principles of Microeconomics) and Econ 2100 (Economics and Policy Problems) and as graduate teaching assistant across the Economics department (Econ 2105, 3110, 4180 game theory, 4357 law and economics, 4520 economics of sports, 7023 PhD econometrics II). One of eight inaugural Graduate Teaching Fellows at Georgia Tech's Center for Teaching and Learning, 2018-19. Instructor at Campus / Campuswire (online university) since July 2022, teaching mathematics and microeconomics for non-traditional and low-income students.

Senator of the Year at Georgia Tech Graduate Student Government in 2020. Vice President for Campus Services 2019-2020; coauthored a white paper on graduate student financial security and health outcomes, advocated for the removal of special institutional fees, and worked on the campus transition to remote operations during COVID-19. Public-Policy Thesis Competition finalist at Barcelona GSE 2015. Third-degree black belt and instructor in Sin Moo HapKiDo since 2007 (Fayetteville, North Carolina).

Languages: English (native), Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian. The Russian and Ukrainian shape the joint work with Dr. Elizaveta Gonchar (his fiancée and most frequent co-author) on the [Ukraine wartime reorganization paper](/research/gonchar-helfrich-2026-ukraine) and the broader Penumbra applications to sanctions and shadow trade.
