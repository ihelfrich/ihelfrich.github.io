Version 0.1 is one complete vertical slice rather than an empty curriculum shell. Its two-group inference module provides purpose-based **Learn it / Run it / Defend it / Teach it** routes; compares pooled, Welch, and binary OLS with HC2; and mechanically verifies the exact coefficient and standard-error equivalences.

The module keeps estimation separate from identification. Its browser lab defaults to descriptive language for observational assignment and makes causal wording conditional on the design that would support it.

The evidence base includes a deterministic synthetic fixture and an archived, attributed World Bank public-data case comparing 2024 life expectancy across low-income and upper-middle-income economies. JavaScript, Python, R, and Julia implementations are executed in the release gate. Excel and Stata versions are not claimed because they are not yet included and mechanically tested.
