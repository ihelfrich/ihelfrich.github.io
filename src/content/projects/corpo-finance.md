This archived build is retained as evidence of an interaction pattern, not presented as a client project. A fictional competitive setting connects corporate-takeover and capital-structure decisions to MBA-level quantitative methods, including Hamada beta, Modigliani-Miller with taxes, Newton-Raphson yield-to-maturity calculations, and Black-Scholes foundations. Its reusable elements belong in the broader Business, Strategy, and Marketing capability family.
