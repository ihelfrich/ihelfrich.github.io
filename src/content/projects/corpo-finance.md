Built for an MBA Corporate Finance tutoring client who needed a reference site that actually engaged with the material instead of looking like every other slide deck. The cyberpunk framing turns out to make the corporate-takeover and capital-structure material more memorable, which is the whole pedagogical bet. The math is honest MBA-level corporate finance (Hamada beta, M&M with taxes, Newton-Raphson YTM, Black-Scholes basics).
