Quarto-rendered book covering the methods I actually use in applied causal-inference work, organized as eight lecture series. The differentiating chapters are 13 (networks and peer effects), 14 (CDEs, mutually guaranteed societies, and pass-through structures), and 15 (optimal transport and effective distance). Every methods chapter pairs R (fixest, did, rdrobust, grf) with Stata (reghdfe, csdid, rdrobust, weakivtest) so readers can use whichever their employer prefers.

The site is also the home for a 32-problem practice set and three fieldwork playbooks built around a Portugal + Ireland blended-finance research trip.
