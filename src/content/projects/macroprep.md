Quarto book covering Case-Fair-Oster Ch. 7–13 and 19. The novelty is the live-data layer: six dashboards pulling 26 cached FRED series (refreshed daily by a GitHub Action) and 100 RSS items from the Fed, BLS, BEA, Treasury, ECB, BoE, IMF, BIS, and EIA. The five interactive Observable JS tools (Keynesian cross, multipliers, money creation, bond pricer, PPP calculator) let students manipulate the parameters rather than reading static plots.
