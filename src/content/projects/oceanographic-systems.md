I develop computational tools that connect marine environments, survey questions, and scientific models in a shared spatial view.

- **See the environment.** Combine bathymetry, terrain meshes, and GIS layers to inspect underwater relief alongside survey context.
- **Understand current fields.** Visualize monthly current climatology and particle advection. Along-track corrections use annual-mean currents to examine travel-time feasibility.
- **Connect acoustic evidence.** Package BELLHOP model outputs with units and source provenance, then prepare explicit spatial transforms for inspection in 3D environments, including Unreal.

This work is in progress. The current-field examples use climatology, not live forecasts. The visualizations support research and survey planning; they are not navigation systems. Imported model outputs remain distinct from observations and field validation, and visualization does not replace the acoustic solver.
