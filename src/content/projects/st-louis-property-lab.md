An interactive city model with building and street geometry, property inspection and scenario comparisons. The application combines City and County spatial coverage with official City parcel evidence where available; a mapped building does not imply a complete property record.

Rental scenarios use explicit assumptions. They are not appraisals, verified listings or forecasts of realized returns. Source and coverage distinctions accompany the relevant controls.

The [spatial portfolio](/spatial/) connects this application to investment geography, the Asheville incentive scenario and LiDAR/terrain work.
