The paper extends Brockmann-Helbing (2013) effective distance from epidemiological networks to economic networks, then validates the metric against a panel of bilateral trade flows under the 2022–2024 Russia sanctions regime. The headline empirical result: effective distance reduces the residual error in the bilateral-trade gravity equation by roughly 18% relative to a great-circle distance specification, with the gain concentrated in country-pairs separated by intermediating third-party trade corridors.

Implications for sanctions policy and supply-chain risk modeling are developed in a final section. The dataset behind the paper is the EffDist V2026 grid, released separately.
