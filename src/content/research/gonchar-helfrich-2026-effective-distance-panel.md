This planned extension of [Trade in the Spotlight](/research/gonchar-helfrich-2025-spotlight) (SSRN 5202676) would add a transport-network layer, structural-gravity validation with three-way FE PPML, and applications to recent chokepoint shocks.

The motivation is that canonical bilateral distance measures are static, while population, infrastructure, and transport corridors change. Open satellite data and transport networks make a time-varying construction feasible to investigate.

The intended outputs are a documented panel, reproducible construction code, and validation results. Coverage, release venue, and row counts remain to be verified before publication.
