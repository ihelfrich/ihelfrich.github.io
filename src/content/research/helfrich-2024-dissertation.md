The dissertation established the longer research arc behind the current work on trade networks, spatial measurement, and optimal transport.

The first chapter augments gravity models with measures of a country's position in the international trade network, using BACI-CEPII data from 1995 through 2015. The second constructs bilateral distance measures from the internal geography of population and nightlight intensity, with applications to U.S. interstate trade in 2017 and global trade from 2015 through 2020. The third develops a theoretical connection between equilibrium and optimal transport in infinite-dimensional spaces.
