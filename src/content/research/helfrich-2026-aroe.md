Theory draft; companion to the proposed **Paper 2 (Russian seaborne crude, 2021-2024)** empirical design. Authorship configuration is under review.

The draft investigates whether a half-order stability rate can be established when the cost is itself a functional of the measure. The proposed argument runs through $c$-convex stability on the best-response map rather than displacement convexity; this remains a draft claim pending proof and authorship review.
