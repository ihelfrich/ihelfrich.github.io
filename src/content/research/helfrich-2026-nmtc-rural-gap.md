Ian Helfrich originated the project and is its sole author. **Contributors:** Katia Antunes and Elizaveta Gonchar. This contributor acknowledgment does not assign or imply specific roles.

The paper makes three contributions. First, it constructs a project-level panel from 19,907 QLICI transactions covering 8,024 projects with origination years 2001–2022, linked to issuing CDEs and receiving QALICBs, with rural/non-metro tract flags from OMB and ERS definitions. Second, it reports a descriptive, noncausal leverage-gap decomposition with CDE fixed effects: most of the explained movement is associated with observed CDE composition, while the within-CDE rural coefficient is not statistically distinguishable from zero. The design does not identify why CDEs differ. Third, it tests for bunching at the 20 percent non-metro administrative target using realized cumulative-deployment data. No excess mass is detected around the target; allocation-agreement compliance is unobserved, so the test does not establish whether the target binds through those agreements. A subgroup scan across twenty-four cells leaves one exception after a multiple-comparison correction—rural commercial real-estate rehabilitation at −0.439—reported as exploratory because that dimension was added after an earlier draft flagged real estate.

The results motivate evaluating intermediary-allocation levers alongside project-level interventions. Because the design is descriptive and the mechanism remains unresolved, it does not establish a causal or lower-cost policy fix.
