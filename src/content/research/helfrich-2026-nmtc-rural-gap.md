[Read the August 2026 working paper (PDF)](/research/ian-helfrich-nmtc-working-paper.pdf) · [Inspect the public repository and evidence viewer](https://github.com/ihelfrich/us-nmtc-viewer)

The paper makes three contributions. First, it constructs a project-level panel from 19,907 QLICI transactions covering 8,024 projects with origination years 2001–2022, linked to issuing CDEs and receiving QALICBs, with rural/non-metro tract flags from OMB and ERS definitions. Second, it reports a descriptive, noncausal leverage-gap decomposition with CDE fixed effects: most of the explained movement is associated with observed CDE composition. The median estimate is near zero and neither margin estimate is statistically distinguishable from zero; the mean interval is wide, so the design does not establish that a within-CDE component is absent or identify why CDEs differ. Third, it tests for bunching at the 20 percent non-metro administrative target using realized cumulative-deployment data. No excess mass is detected around the target; allocation-agreement compliance is unobserved, so the test does not establish whether the target binds through those agreements. A subgroup scan across twenty-four cells leaves one exception after a multiple-comparison correction—rural commercial real-estate rehabilitation at −0.439—reported as exploratory because that dimension was added after an earlier draft flagged real estate.

The results motivate evaluating intermediary-allocation levers alongside project-level interventions. Because the design is descriptive and the mechanism remains unresolved, it does not establish a causal or lower-cost policy fix.
