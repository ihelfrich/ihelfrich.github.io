Ian Helfrich's course connects the theory of stochastic processes with the decisions a forecaster must defend. It develops stationary models, forecasting, economic measurement, integrated processes, dynamic regression, state-space filtering, VARs, structural identification, cointegration, conditional volatility, and model revision.
